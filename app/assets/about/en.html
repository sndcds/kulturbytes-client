Find events, venues and cultural offerings

kulturbytes helps you find events, cultural venues and cultural offerings that match your interests. Our goal is to make cultural information more accessible and bring the diversity of cultural life together in one place. kulturbytes therefore provides an open platform where cultural institutions, projects, initiatives and event organizers can publish their offerings. Visitors can search, filter and discover this information free of charge – simply, clearly and without barriers.

Do you organize events?

With a free account, you can publish your offerings on kulturbytes and make them visible to visitors.

Open, diverse and inclusive

kulturbytes is a platform for culture and cultural participation. Our goal is to make cultural offerings accessible to as many people as possible and to foster exchange between cultural practitioners, institutions and visitors.

kulturbytes is not a platform for party-political communication. Events whose primary focus is party-political advertising or party-political opinion-forming are therefore not included in the event calendar. This rule applies regardless of the party or political direction concerned.

kulturbytes stands for a diverse, open and democratic cultural life. We want to make access to cultural offerings easier regardless of origin, age, gender, identity, disability, religion or worldview, and promote cultural participation. Respect, openness and mutual recognition are the foundation of our work.
